How to play

TempRace is played on one phone that gets passed around. Two teams, a wheel picks who types, random letters appear, and you write a word for each one against the clock. Then you hand the phone to a rival from the other team, who has to guess what you wrote. Every hit scores.

Inside the app, the How to play button on the home screen walks through the whole flow with examples.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need internet to play?

No. The game works completely offline. Internet is only needed to show ads in the free version and to process purchases.

I bought Premium but still see ads

Open the app, tap the gold crown to reach the paywall, and tap Restore purchase. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID you bought with. If it still happens, email me and I will look into it.

I got a new phone. Do I lose Premium?

No. Sign in with the same Apple ID and tap Restore purchase. Both the lifetime purchase and the subscription are tied to your account, not to the device.

How do I cancel the monthly subscription?

On your iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → TempRace Premium → Cancel. Apple handles cancellations directly; I cannot do it for you.

My game history disappeared

History is stored on your device and synced to your iCloud if you have it enabled. Check that iCloud Drive is turned on in Settings.

I want to request a feature

Write to me. The ideas that come from people actually playing are the ones that end up in the app.
