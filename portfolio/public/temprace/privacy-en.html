TempRace Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 4, 2026

TempRace is a party game played by several people on a single phone. This policy explains what data the app handles and what we do with it.

In short: TempRace does not collect personal data, has no ads, uses no analytics or tracking tools, and does not share information with third parties. Everything you type stays on your device and, if you have it enabled, in your own iCloud account.

What information the app handles

TempRace only stores what you type in order to play:

  • The player and team names you enter when setting up a game.
  • Your game history (scores, rounds played, points per player and game mode), if you have the Premium version.
  • Your preferences inside the app: light or dark theme, haptics and game settings.

Player names are free text: you can use nicknames or initials if you would rather not use real names. The app does not verify them or link them to any identity.

Where that information is stored

All of it is stored on your device. If you have iCloud enabled, your game history is also synced to your own iCloud account so you can see it from your other Apple devices.

That sync is handled by Apple and the data stays inside your account: the developer of TempRace never has access to it. You can read how Apple handles that information in Apple's Privacy Policy.

What TempRace does NOT do

  • It does not ask for or store your real name, email address, phone number or location.
  • It does not access your contacts, photos, microphone or camera.
  • It does not show ads.
  • It does not use analytics, usage statistics or tracking identifiers.
  • It does not send your information to any servers of ours: the app works offline.
  • It does not sell or share information with third parties.

In-app purchases

TempRace offers a Premium version. Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. The developer never receives or sees your payment details: the app only asks Apple whether the purchase is active in order to unlock the features. Handling of that data is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Children

TempRace is suitable for all ages and does not collect personal data from any user, including children under 13.

How to delete your data

Uninstalling the app removes all information stored on the device. To also delete what was synced to iCloud, go to Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage and delete TempRace's data.

Changes to this policy

If TempRace ever adds features that handle information differently — for example, an online multiplayer mode — this policy will be updated before that version ships, and the new date will be shown here.
